# Focused improvement plan

## Evidence versus hypotheses

The [feedback sample](feedback.md) contains two issues from one GitHub tracker. Every theme is thin. There is no basis for claiming a ranked market-wide complaint list, willingness to pay, or that Kivou is better than every alternative.

| Improvement | Basis | Delivery decision | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clear portable Windows guide | Upstream issue #12909 and our Windows-first target | Explain exact artifacts, model prerequisites and update limitations | Test the documented steps against our built ZIP |
| Visible recent-run state and recovery | Approved creator workflow plus history-action report #11529 | Show statuses and results without making deletion necessary | Keyboard, narrow-window and restart checks |
| Three guided creator workflows | Approved user product direction | Research Brief, Content Pack, Asset Manifest | Real creator task completion; not just screenshots |
| Reviewable sources and exports | Approved product direction | Explicit provenance and separate immutable delivery folders | Missing-evidence, partial-failure and no-overwrite tests |
| One active model run | Observed test machine has roughly 8 GB RAM; zero extra-spend constraint | Avoid parallel model processes and automatic paid fallback | Measured memory, duration and cancellation behavior |

## Positioning to test

**Kivou helps independent creators turn a rough idea into a reviewable content pack, using their own model access and local delivery files.**

This is a claim about intended capability, not proven demand, guaranteed productivity, income or privacy beyond the documented data path. Keep that distinction in the README, demo and release notes.
